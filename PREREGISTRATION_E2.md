## 1. Population and payloads

- Anchors: `corpus/e2_dilution/anchors_frozen.jsonl` — **959 anchor sets** (of 1,273 candidates carrying original + paraphrase + affirmative edits) where BOTH the paraphrase (edit 1) and affirmative/reversal (edit 3) are single-sentence *substitutions* against the original passage, per the pinned diff algorithm (`harness/e2/build_anchors.py`; **stricter than** the estimator in `scripts/verify_condaqa.py` by one predicate — the estimator counts any single changed slot including insertions/deletions, the builder requires a 1:1 substitution. The two counts are 960 vs 959; the sole divergent candidate is **train-119**, whose paraphrase edit is a whole-sentence 0:1 *insertion* — no original counterpart, no slot position, cannot satisfy the byte-identical-host/identical-position requirement below — correctly excluded. Two-source check reported here per PLAN §1.2; yields 75.4% as single-slot changes, **75.3% as strict same-slot substitutions**, and the same-slot rate 1.0 is a property of the 959). 903 also carry the scope edit as the high-overlap flip control. Splits 363/86/510 (train/dev/test); split membership is recorded but pooled for the titration (CondaQA's QA splits are irrelevant to pairwise gate decisions; no model is trained).
- Pair classes per anchor: **flip** = (payload_orig → payload_affirm), **faithful** = (payload_orig → payload_para), **scope** (secondary) = (payload_orig → payload_scope).
- Every anchor's paraphrase and affirmative edits modify the SAME sentence slot (measured rate 1.0), so both members of every pair share byte-identical host context with the payload at an identical position.

## 2. Design

- **Length axis, defined.** L is the **nominal bin label**, a construction unit: each titrated passage is assembled once to W(L) = ⌈L/1.3⌉ whitespace tokens and shared **byte-identically across all encoders** (per-encoder bins would destroy the byte-identity of the stimulus set and multiply the corpus tenfold). Bins L ∈ {64, 96, 128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048, 4096} (the 96 bin added 2026-08-08 against the intercept-bin attrition cliff). The per-(encoder, passage) **realized token count under the encoder's own tokenizer is recorded**, its per-bin median reported as a secondary axis; all L\* values are stated on the nominal axis with realized-token medians alongside. **Truncation-regime membership is decided per (encoder, passage) by realized tokens > effective window** (window arithmetic **includes special tokens**, computed and tabled before the tag); at the boundary bin L=512, the fraction of passages exceeding each 512-window is reported and that bin is analyzed both including and excluding them.
- **SPLICE arm (primary instrument).** The payload sentence is embedded in real host text at controlled position ∈ {early, middle, late} (first/middle/last decile of sentence slots), titrated across the L grid. **Drop rule, pinned:** an **anchor** drops from bin L when max(whitespace tokens of payload_orig, payload_para, payload_affirm) > W(L)/2, where W(L) = ⌈L/1.3⌉ is the bin's whitespace construction budget — anchor-level by definition, so flip and faithful cells always contain identical anchor sets. Measured attrition on the frozen anchors (max-member): the unit and member-set choices move the L=64 drop between 21.6% and 61.4%, which is why they are pinned here and not left to the harness; per-bin n and the dropped anchors' payload-length distribution are reported per cell. **Cohorts:** the *constant cohort* (anchors eligible at every fitted bin) is **co-primary** for all decay fits and cross-L comparisons alongside the full per-bin population; a qualitative disagreement between the two curve families is itself a reported finding. The cohort n under the pinned rule is stated in §6 before the tag; if it lands below 400, the L=96 bin (not L=64) becomes the fit floor for the constant cohort and the fact is disclosed.
- **Host assignment (pinned before tag).** Which enwiki articles qualify as hosts, how many host draws per (anchor, L, position), the RNG seed, and the contamination exclusion — a host must not contain the payload sentence or the anchor's source passage — are specified in §6 at tag time; hosts are drawn once and shared byte-identically across all encoders. Hosts: **enwiki-20210701** (fit domain; frozen, sha256 `cf5ab6b3…`), **PMC-OA CC-BY slice** (held-out 1; **pin and freeze NOW from `s3://pmc-oa-opendata/deprecated/oa_comm/txt/all/` — the deprecated prefix is deleted on or after 2026-08-24** (NCBI announcement 2026-02-12); slice = ascending PMC ID over the AWS OA `oa_comm` txt listing — the listing itself snapshotted into MANIFEST at retrieval — body ≥ 6,000 **whitespace** tokens, first 2,000 qualifying articles, no other exclusions; license file lists frozen with sha256s; post-expiry reproduction route recorded as the eSearch→S3 per-article pipeline), **RFC text corpus** (held-out 2; frozen shared snapshot with E1; spliced RFC derivatives are never redistributed — regeneration script + checksums only).
- **NATIVE arm (supporting, ecological).** The CondaQA passage extended with its actual surrounding article text from the pinned dump; expected substring-match rate 70–90%, disclosed; failures drop from NATIVE only. CondaQA's README says only "a July 2021 version" of Wikipedia; before mining, a 50-passage sample is tested against **both** July 2021 dumps (20210701, 20210720) and the winner is recorded — "exact source snapshot" is an inference until then.
- **Encoders, numerics, and device pins (fixed pre-sweep).** The nine pinned polaritycheck configurations + long-context nomic at its full window (the largest titrated bin is 4096; window headroom beyond that is reported as unused rather than promised). **E2 scores on the pinned polaritycheck reference numerics carried from Paper A/E1 — not E3's fresh-fp32 regime — per the D4 ruling line ("gte/mxbai CPU-only per the parity ruling").** gte-base and mxbai-embed-large are fp16-native and **CPU-only on the pinned Wu-CPU path**; the remaining seven pinned configs may run on GPU, **one pinned device per config, fixed here: Forge (3090): bge, e5 [query:], e5 [no prefix], nomic-clustering, nomic-MRL-256 · Agora (16 GB): MiniLM, mpnet.** Long-context nomic is a **new named configuration** outside the Paper A registry: Forge, fp32, checkpoint revision pinned at tag time. Before any shard is admitted, each GPU-assigned config re-passes the frozen-probe parity gate (cosine delta ≤ 1e-5 vs the pinned path; probe set extends `results/verification/h4_gpu_parity_probes.json` with titrated-length probes at L ∈ {64, 512, 4096}). Shards merge only through `merge_shards.py --verify-config-hash`, where the hash covers checkpoint hash + device + dtype + library version; a shard bearing an unpinned triple is rejected. A device failure re-pins by dated amendment below the tag (E3 amendment A1 precedent). *Wall-clock note for the operator: the two CPU-only configs put multi-day Wu-CPU time on the critical path at this grid; if that binds, the E3 precedent (a fresh-numerics pin for a fresh experiment) is available by ruling — but it is a ruling, not a default.*
- **Measurements** per (encoder, L, position, host, arm): flip-vs-faithful decision AUROC over anchors; cosine-gap Δ distributions; **both gate error directions** at operating points {0.30, 0.40, 0.60, 0.80, 0.85, 0.95} — flip *miss* rate AND faithful *false-block* rate (a gate that converges to constant-pass shows miss→1 with false-block→0; reporting one direction cannot distinguish blindness from degeneracy). Operating-point provenance: the five shipped points of Paper A §5 plus the audited guard's cosine-0.60 equivalent (Paper A §6).
- **Fits.** **Δ(L) = Δ₀·(64/L)^α** per encoder on Wikipedia hosts — k is pinned to 64 so Δ₀ ≡ Δ(64), the measured intercept (the three-parameter form Δ₀·(k/L)^α is unidentifiable: only Δ₀·k^α enters the likelihood). A monotone-spline nonparametric curve is fit alongside; **the spline is the L\* carrier** (the power law is the parametric summary). Fit domain: within-window bins only (truncation bins excluded, listed per encoder); a parametric fit is attempted only where ≥ 5 within-window bins exist, else spline-only. **L\* definitions:** dilution-L\*(A) = first within-window crossing of AUROC = A on the fitted spline (A ∈ {0.75, 0.55}); truncation-L\* = the window edge, reported as a separate named quantity, never pooled with dilution-L\*. Censoring codes are reported, never extrapolated values: "≤ 64" (already at or below the level at the first bin), "> 4096" (no crossing in domain), "> window" (crossing would sit in the truncation regime). Every reported L\* carries an anchor-level bootstrap CI (refit per resample). Gate-term critical length (named descriptive): smallest L at which flip miss rate ≥ 0.99 at every operating point ≤ 0.85.

## 3. Hypotheses and bars

Primary endpoints are the H-numbered bars below, each with its aggregation stated; the full measurement grid is descriptive and carries no pass/fail. All CIs and tests bootstrap over anchors (the repeated unit), never over cells.

- **Scoring convention for every bar:** "below X **by** L=B" means the quantity is ≤ X at bin B **and at every larger within-window bin**; monotonicity violations are reported per cell, never smoothed. The **membership lists** — which of the nine count as mean-pooled (H1), and which configurations the NevIR at/below-random set names (external anchor) — are enumerated in §6 at tag time, not left implicit.
- **H1 (calibration, not discovery):** α ∈ [0.8, 1.2] for the mean-pooled set — the near-arithmetic dilution of a one-sentence perturbation; deviations by CLS-pooling/attention encoders are reported as findings. **Sharp form:** the arithmetic lexical decay Δ_lex(L) (token-Jaccard gap under the pinned tokenizer) is overlaid and α_encoder is tested against α_lex = 1; an encoder whose α departs from its own lexical arithmetic is the finding.
- **H2 (the headline — universal failure length), three separately scored clauses** (bundled, one clause's failure would poison the others' clean passes):
  - **H2a:** every configuration, including the bge/mxbai class (AUROC 0.79–0.90 at sentence scale in Paper A — the named-set figure, strongest two), falls below flip-vs-faithful AUROC 0.60 by L=512 on the **SPLICE arm, raw** (primary).
  - **H2b:** below 0.65 by L=1024, caliper-matched.
  - **H2c:** the production nomic-MRL-256 path ≤ 0.55 **at every bin including L=64** (95% CI containing or below 0.55 in every within-window SPLICE cell). *Scoring note: the lab's own E3 result (production path 0.679 on PAWS at sentence scale) marks the short-L end of this clause as a real risk; the NevIR anchor is the stated defense; a short-L failure here is reported as such, not absorbed.*
  - **Intersection test for H2a/H2b:** per-config anchor-bootstrap 95% upper bound below the bar — never raw point estimates (at per-cell n, point-estimate intersections fail by noise with better-than-even odds across ten configs). The NATIVE arm is confirmatory-ecological for the same bars.
- **H3a (position, within window):** at L ≤ window/2 per encoder (so boundary clipping cannot contaminate the cell), AUROC(early) − AUROC(late) ≥ 0.05 for ≥ half the configurations — metric pinned to AUROC (threshold-free; miss-rate deltas collide with H4's ceiling: pooled miss ≥ 0.95 caps any position delta at 0.05).
- **H3b (truncation, reported deployment fact — not a hypothesis):** late-position flips beyond a 512-window encoder's effective window are missed at rate 1.0 by construction; reported as deployment behavior with per-encoder effective-window arithmetic, carrying zero confirmatory weight.
- **H4 (shipped thresholds):** on the SPLICE arm, positions pooled: at cosine 0.85, flip miss rate ≥ 0.95 for every configuration at L ≥ 256; at 0.95, the contradicting passage is judged redundant — i.e., misses at the 0.95 operating point — ≥ 90% at L ≥ 128. **Degeneracy twin (named descriptive):** by L ≥ 256 at thresholds ≤ 0.85, the gate's two error rates converge to (miss → 1, false-block → 0) — the gate is not misjudging pairs, it has become a constant.
- **H5 (confounder starvation — calibration, not discovery):** |r(whole-passage token-Jaccard, class)| < 0.10 by L=512, r = point-biserial Pearson of whole-passage token-Jaccard against the binary class, same "by" semantics as H2. In the SPLICE arm this is near-arithmetic (byte-identical hosts confine class information to the payload; r decays as O(1/L) by construction), so the observed r(L) is reported **against its closed-form prediction** computed from the payload-Jaccard distributions; the deviation, not the decay, is the finding. This is an additional, independent reason overlap-conditioned repair is information-starved at deployment lengths — **distinct from** Paper A §7's cross-author instability, which was measured at sentence scale where dilution is absent (0.750 → 0.533 held-out). The non-arithmetic version of this quantity lives in E1 (r at section-context granularity on natural errata) and is registered there as a post-hoc amendment.
- **H6 (transfer):** Wikipedia-fitted decay curves predict PMC and RFC AUROC. **Registered criterion over the 140 held-out cells (10 configs × 7 bins × 2 hosts): PASS = ≥ 126/140 (90%) inside the frozen curves' 95% prediction bands AND no single configuration with > 3 of its 14 cells outside; every miss is listed.** (Pointwise per-cell scoring guarantees ~7 chance failures under perfect transfer; that failure mode is excluded by construction.)
- **External anchor (NevIR):** the configurations NevIR's published results score at/below random on passage-scale negation ranking (set enumerated in §6 at tag time, with citation) show **Δ(64) < 0.05** — the intercept is externally anchored at the measured first bin (not at a fit parameter); NevIR's pairwise-accuracy metric is reported on the L=64 cell as a bridge baseline.
- **Exploratory arm (non-binding, operator ruling pending):** publicly released negation-aware encoder checkpoints (Truong et al. 2025 at minimum, if obtainable) run through the same grid as configurations 10+, labeled exploratory, outside every bar above — closing the "you never test the repair you cite as promising" review before it is written. Their deep evaluation (survival of reported gains under overlap matching) stays deferred to Paper C.
