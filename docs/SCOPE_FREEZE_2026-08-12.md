# SCOPE FREEZE — 2026-08-12

**No further experiments before submission. No new corpora. No new claims above the evidence.**

## Why this file exists

Every failure in this program today occurred in the synthesis layer, not the measurement layer.
The experiments were pre-registered, frozen, and replicable, and they held. What failed, twice in
one day, was a headline claim assembled on top of them: the sign-flip law, and before it the
motivation for the Floor Lemma. In both cases the pre-registration discipline caught the
overreach, which is the discipline working. What did not work was the response, which each time
was to look for a stronger law rather than to write the paper at the altitude the evidence
already supported.

That loop does not converge. It is the pattern that killed Paper C.

## What is frozen as of now

**The evidence.** E1, E2, E3, E4, the two label audits, the cascade, and the cross-source
construct test are complete, frozen, and will not be added to before submission. Twenty-one
pre-registered bars, all scored, ledgered one row each in `docs/bar_ledger.md`.

**The claim altitude.** The paper reports what was measured at the operating points that ship,
against labels the investigators did not author, and it releases the instruments. It does not
carry a named law. Specifically:

- The coupling **magnitude** difference between errata and bis is reported as a two-corpus result
  inside one standards body, with its selection-effect check attached, and it is not generalized
  to authoring processes at large.
- The **sign-flip law is retired**. Its inverted row described a designed 2×2, not a text source.
- **"Benchmarking cannot certify"** is retired as a universal. What is reported is the exhibited
  case: a pre-registered transfer prediction that failed in the direction that looks like success.

**The remaining work is writing, not measuring.** Restate the lead, retitle, rewrite the abstract,
fold Related Work, apply the figures, run one manuscript review, submit.

## The three standards, assessed

- **Replicability: met.** Every number recomputes from a frozen JSON by a named script; figures
  generate from those JSONs rather than from prose; 31 references verified against primary
  sources; two ledgers (bars, corrections) exist so no arithmetic or superseded value can drift
  back in.
- **Review: met, arguably over-met.** A five-lens design review, a hostile manuscript review, a
  132-claim number-fidelity audit, a 25-item reference-characterization pass, and a
  pre-registered refutation test that went against the paper's own lead claim.
- **Writing: not yet met.** The manuscript still leads with a retired claim. This is the only
  standard currently failing, and it is repaired by writing, not by another experiment.

## The rule going forward

A claim enters the manuscript only if it is the direct reading of a frozen result. If stating it
requires a generalization the data does not contain, it does not go in, and no new experiment is
commissioned to rescue it. Anything that would be interesting to test next is Paper C material and
is written down as such, not run.
