Semantic Flowstructures

A sentence drawn as a physical object: grammatical components become flow primitives — sources, conduits, valves, regulators, gauges — and the composed shape is the flowstructure. The vernacular exists to make one thing visible: two sentences can share almost every part of their silhouette while their flows run in opposite directions. The gate class we audit compares silhouettes. Meaning lives in the valves.

Every cosine printed below is a measured number from the frozen results, cited beneath it. Nothing is illustrative.
