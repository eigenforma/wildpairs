## What each verdict means

- **HE3-2 PASS (9/9):** every configuration ≥ 0.80 naive on QQP (0.85–0.90). Cosine looks
  excellent exactly where the coupling is aligned — the field's evidence base for "cosine works"
  is dominated by corpora of this tier.
- **HE3-1 FAIL (0/9), the finding:** the Jaccard-caliper removes only 0.07–0.10 of QQP's
  separability and 0.02–0.05 of SNLI/MNLI's. **Overlap-matching within an elicited corpus does
  not produce a valid instrument** — elicited classes differ in surface style beyond token overlap
  (the NLI hypothesis-only-artifact literature names the mechanism: contradiction cues), and
  cosine reads that style. Only true minimal-pair construction — PAWS's scrambles, the errata's
  corrections, CondaQA's edits — removes the full artifact. This *extends* Paper A §3: the
  confounder is not just lexical overlap; it is any class-correlated surface property, and
  Jaccard is merely its most visible face. The paper's methodological claim sharpens: **the valid
  instrument criterion is minimal-pairhood, not overlap balance.**
- **HE3-3 FAIL (production 0.679 > 0.62):** all nine inside [0.50, 0.75] on PAWS, but the
  production MRL-256 path scored *best* of the nine — at sentence scale on scramble-adversarial
  pairs it is less blind than Paper A's errata/sentence results predicted. Reported as a
  wrong prediction; consistent with heterogeneous per-regime encoder behavior (Paper A §5).
- **HE3-4 FAIL (28 cells > 0.70 BA):** shipped thresholds genuinely function on aligned corpora
  (up to ~0.8 balanced accuracy at cosine 0.80/0.85 on QQP/SNLI). **Our own sign-flip law beat
  our bar**: where the coupling aligns, single-cosine gates work; the bar wrongly assumed Paper
  A's ceiling was regime-independent. This is the law's strongest confirmation in the E3 data —
  gate performance is a function of the corpus's coupling orientation, not of the gate.
- **HE3-5 FAIL (1/9):** naive AUROC does not rank by coupling magnitude (SNLI ≥ QQP despite
  weaker δ) — coupling magnitude predicts the *artifact*, not the *total* separability, which
  also carries elicitation style. Prediction retired; the refined statement is HE3-1's.
