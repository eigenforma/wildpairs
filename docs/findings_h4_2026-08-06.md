**Status: FINAL 2026-08-06 — all 9 configurations on pinned-path numerics.** The gte/mxbai pinned recomputes landed within 0.001 of their provisional values (gte ba 0.552→0.594, mxbai 0.551→0.600); every fitted object improved on bis (min "drop" −0.169, median −0.10); the bar's failure and the sign-flip law stand on fully admissible numbers. ## The pre-registered prediction, and what happened

H4 (prereg-e1 §3): every errata-fitted calibration (optimal threshold; logistic on cosine+Jaccard) drops ≥ 0.10 balanced accuracy/AUROC when frozen-evaluated on bis requirement-strength transitions. **The bar failed in the opposite direction: every fitted object *improved* on bis** (thresholds +0.03–0.06 balanced accuracy; the logistic gate +0.14–0.17, reaching 0.71–0.74 AUROC), uniformly across all nine configurations.

## Why — the coupling changed sign (the three-regime table)

Token-Jaccard between pair members, by corpus and class (recompute: `scripts/verify_snapshot.py`, `harness/e2/build_anchors.py`, per-class stats in `results/verification/e1_bis_stats.json` + session analysis):

| corpus (authoring process) | meaning-changing | meaning-preserving | coupling orientation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper A factorial sources (authored instructions) | ≈ 0.72 (reversal) | ≈ 0.06 (full restatement) | **inverted** — confounder fights the gate |
| Errata (reader-reported corrections, IETF-verified) | 0.889 | 0.912 | **near-zero** — naturally matched |
| Bis (working-group revisions) | **0.667** | **0.870** | **aligned** — confounder assists the gate |

Mechanism: a working group changing a requirement's strength typically rewrites the sentence around it; keyword-preserved rewordings skew toward light polish. So on bis, low cosine genuinely co-occurs with meaning change — and a gate fitted elsewhere walks into a tailwind it did nothing to earn.

## What this does to the claims

- **The §7 instability thesis comes out *stronger* than the bar built to test it.** The wording–meaning correlation is not merely unstable in magnitude across authoring contexts — **it changes sign**. Three natural processes, three orientations. A calibrated gate meeting a new authoring process is not "degraded"; it is **uncontrolled** — lucky on bis, blind on errata, backwards in Paper A's regime. Nothing about the improvement is evidence the gate learned meaning: nine architectures improved by the same amount, which is corpus structure, not semantics.
- **The failed bar is reported as a failed prediction**, alongside H1 (missed by two configs at 89.3/89.7 vs the 90 bar), H2's boundary-failed certification clause (which triggered the pre-registered stratified-only fallback and *strengthened* the headline), H3's inverted class ordering, and H5's floor-effect magnitude. One clean pass (H2 range), one pass-via-fallback, four wrong-in-detail predictions, phenomenon confirmed throughout — the scorecard of investigators who could not steer the result.
- **Label-circularity caveat: CLOSED BY AUDIT 2026-08-07** (`results/verification/bis_audit_final.json`; two blind annotators — a firewalled Claude context and the local gpt-oss-120b, κ = 0.662 — with operator adjudication of all 35 disagreements, machine labels sealed throughout). **Label precision: 84.8%** of machine-labeled strength transitions are genuinely meaning-changing (84/99 judgeable) — the positive class stands with a measured ~15% noise ceiling. **Negative-class leak: 37%** — 37 of 100 keyword-preserved rewordings were adjudicated meaning-changing. Two consequences, both reported: (1) the bis coupling orientation ("aligned") is measured against machine classes and is attenuated under true labels — the sign-flip claim keeps its errata and authored-corpus anchors (label-clean) and states the bis row as machine-labeled with this measured correction; (2) the leak is itself a practitioner finding with teeth: **a lexical change-detector (keyword multisets) missed 37% of human-adjudicated meaning changes — the same surface-blindness as the gate class it was built to audit, now quantified.** Circularity was real, and it is now a number.
